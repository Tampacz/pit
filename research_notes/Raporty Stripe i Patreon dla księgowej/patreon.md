# Patreon income for a Polish JDG: tax/VAT treatment, documentation and Patreon data for a monthly accounting handover (state as of 2026-10-19)

Method note: support.patreon.com HTML pages return HTTP 403 to automated fetchers. The Patreon Help Center articles cited below were read in full via Patreon's public Zendesk Help Center API (`https://support.patreon.com/api/v2/help_center/articles/search.json?query=...`), which also returns each article's `updated_at` date. Those dates are given below so the reader can judge how current each claim is. The Terms of Use and docs.patreon.com were downloaded directly.

## 1. VAT model: who is the supplier to patrons, which Patreon entity contracts with the creator, and how the creator's supply is treated under Polish VAT (NP / art. 28b, invoices, KSeF, VAT-exempt creator)

### Takeaway
Patreon collects and remits VAT from patrons in its own name, as an online marketplace / deemed supplier. Creators contract with Patreon, Inc. (USA), and all creator payments come from that US entity. For a Polish JDG, the usual reading is that the creator supplies a B2B service to Patreon, Inc. The place of supply is the USA (art. 28b), so the service is outside Polish VAT ("NP") and is not reported in VAT-UE. Under art. 106a pkt 2 lit. b and art. 106b ust. 1 pkt 1, an invoice to Patreon, Inc. appears to be required for both active VAT payers and VAT-exempt taxpayers. In 2026 that invoice falls under mandatory KSeF, apart from the 10,000 PLN/month relief that runs until 31 Dec 2026. The disputed point is Patreon's fees. Patreon says it may charge VAT on fees, or that a VAT-registered creator self-assesses (reverse charge). Polish practitioners treat the fees as import of services, which is neutral for an active VAT payer but a real 23% cost for a VAT-exempt creator.

**Creator's supply under Polish VAT: place of supply, invoice, KSeF**
- Art. 106a ustawy o VAT (legal state 19.10.2026, Dz.U. 2025 poz. 775 t.j.): the invoicing chapter applies to, among others, "2) dostawy towarów i świadczenia usług dokonywanych przez podatnika posiadającego na terytorium kraju siedzibę działalności gospodarczej ... w przypadku gdy miejscem świadczenia jest terytorium: ... b) państwa trzeciego". — [LexLege: art. 106a ustawy o VAT](https://lexlege.pl/ustawa-o-podatku-od-towarow-i-uslug/art-106a/)
- Art. 106b ust. 1 pkt 1: the taxpayer must invoice "sprzedaż, a także dostawę towarów i świadczenie usług, o których mowa w art. 106a pkt 2, dokonywane przez niego na rzecz innego podatnika podatku, podatku od wartości dodanej lub podatku o podobnym charakterze lub na rzecz osoby prawnej niebędącej podatnikiem". Art. 106b ust. 2 waives invoicing only for "sprzedaży zwolnionej od podatku na podstawie art. 43 ust. 1, art. 113 ust. 1 i 9 ...". — [LexLege: art. 106b ustawy o VAT](https://lexlege.pl/ustawa-o-podatku-od-towarow-i-uslug/art-106b/)
- A 2016 interpretation (Dyrektor Izby Skarbowej w Katowicach, for the Minister of Finance) concerned a **VAT-exempt (art. 113) taxpayer providing services to a third-country business**. The taxpayer said services supplied outside Poland do not count toward the exemption limit; the authority called this "prawidłowe". The taxpayer also said no invoice was required; the authority called this **"nieprawidłowe"**, i.e. an invoice obligation exists. The reasoning sits behind a paywall. — [podatki.biz (13.04.2016)](https://www.podatki.biz/artykuly/zwolnienie-podmiotowe-w-vat-uslugi-na-rzecz-kontrahenta-z-kraju-trzeciego_14_31153.htm)
- A tax adviser on B2B services to a US business: the place of supply is the USA (art. 28b), so the service is outside Polish VAT. The invoice should be issued without VAT and annotated with art. 28b / place of supply outside Poland. KSeF marking **"NP1"** applies to non-EU recipients; NP2 is for EU recipients reportable in VAT-UE (art. 100 ust. 1 pkt 4). The transaction is not reported in VAT-UE. The buyer must be a taxable person. (Rafał Styczyński, 05.05.2026) — [Prawo.pl: Świadczenie usług dla zagranicznego kontrahenta z USA a KSeF](https://www.prawo.pl/podatki/swiadczenie-uslug-dla-zagranicznego-kontrahenta-z-usa-a-ksef,1543564.html)
- A creator-tax guide (updated 10.09.2026) says VAT from patrons is settled by Patreon ("VAT od patronów rozlicza sam Patreon"). It says the creator's service to Patreon, Inc. is taxed in the buyer's country and **does not count toward the 240,000 PLN (2026) VAT-exemption limit**. It recommends an invoice to Patreon without VAT. Note: it calls this "odwrotne obciążenie", which differs from the NP/art. 28b annotation in the Prawo.pl article. — [KontoTwórcy.pl: Patreon a podatek (2026)](https://kontotworcy.pl/poradnik/patreon-podatek/)
- An accounting office (page © 2026): payouts from Patreon (US seat) "zwykle" bear no Polish VAT and do not count toward the 240,000 PLN limit. Whether you must issue sales invoices "zależy od Twojego statusu w VAT". — [General Office: rozliczenia Patronite/Patreon](https://generaloffice.pl/rozliczenia-podatkowe-patronite-patreon/)
- KSeF and foreign buyers (secondary sources): an invoice subject to the KSeF obligation must be issued in KSeF even if the buyer has no access to it. A foreign buyer without a Polish NIP receives it by an agreed channel (e.g., PDF by e-mail) bearing a **QR code** that allows verification. — [Infor.pl: KSeF a podmioty zagraniczne](https://ksiegowosc.infor.pl/podatki/vat/faktura/6295034,czy-w-ksef-trzeba-bedzie-wystawiac-efaktury-dla-podmiotow-zagranicznych.html); [PoradnikPrzedsiębiorcy: KSeF a faktury zagraniczne](https://poradnikprzedsiebiorcy.pl/-ksef-a-faktury-zagraniczne-czy-dotyczy-kazdej-sprzedazy) (read only through search snippets)
- KSeF transitional relief (secondary source, search snippet): under **art. 145m**, from 1 April to 31 December 2026, a taxpayer may keep issuing paper or electronic invoices outside KSeF if total monthly sales documented by invoices, **gross**, do not exceed **10,000 PLN**. The right is lost from the invoice that crosses the limit. From 1 January 2027 the relief ends. — [Infor.pl: zwolnienie z KSeF w 2026 r. do 10 tys. zł](https://ksiegowosc.infor.pl/podatki/vat/faktura/7505444,zwolnienie-z-ksef-w-2026-r-przy-miesiecznej-sprzedazy-do-10-tys-zl-jak-liczyc-sprzedaz-od-kiedy-fakturowac-w-ksef-po-przekroczeniu-tego-limitu.html)

- Terms: "Fees charged by Patreon to creators may be subject to VAT ... - Polish practice: "VAT dotyczy za to prowizji, którą Patreon zatrzymuje", treated as **import usług**. A VAT-exempt taxpayer pays 23% on the fee via **VAT-9M by the 25th of the following month**. An active VAT payer reports it in JPK and "zwykle nic nie dopłaca". — [General Office](https://generaloffice.pl/rozliczenia-podatkowe-patronite-patreon/)
- Same view elsewhere: the platform fee invoiced from abroad is settled by the creator as buyer (reverse charge), usually neutral for active VAT payers. Listed errors: adding Polish VAT to the invoice to Patreon, or omitting the fee from VAT settlement. — [KontoTwórcy.pl](https://kontotworcy.pl/poradnik/patreon-podatek/)

### Inferences
- Counterparty for the invoice: **Patreon, Inc., 600 Townsend Street Suite 500, San Francisco, CA, USA**. The Terms name the Irish and UK entities only for processing fans' payments, and Patreon states that creators contract with, and are paid by, Patreon, Inc. If the accountant ever concluded that the EU-fan portion is supplied to Patreon Ireland Limited instead, it would become an EU B2B service: NP, reported in VAT-UE (art. 100 ust. 1 pkt 4), and requiring VAT-UE registration. Patreon's statements do not support that reading, but it should be consciously ruled out.
- **Active VAT payer (czynny):** issue a monthly invoice to Patreon, Inc. without Polish VAT, marked NP / art. 28b (KSeF code NP1 per Prawo.pl). From general knowledge, not verified here: report it in JPK_V7 as a supply outside the country (K_11), not in VAT-UE. Treat Patreon fees as import of services (reverse charge, output and input VAT in the same JPK), which is neutral.
- **VAT-exempt (zwolniony podmiotowo):** the NP service does not use up the 240k PLN limit (podatki.biz 2016 interpretation; KontoTwórcy; General Office). A literal reading of art. 106a pkt 2 lit. b with 106b ust. 1 pkt 1, and the 2016 interpretation, suggest an invoice is still required. The art. 106b ust. 2 waiver covers only "sprzedaż zwolniona", and an NP service abroad is not "sprzedaż" in Poland. In 2026 that invoice is a KSeF invoice unless the 10k PLN/month art. 145m relief applies. If the fees are treated as import of services, VAT-9M at 23% on the fees is a real cost. Rough size: (10% platform + ~3.4% processing + 2.5% conversion if applicable) × 23% ≈ **2.4–3.7% of gross Patreon revenue**. This is my calculation from Patreon's fee tables; see section 6.
- **Conceptual tension to raise with the accountant:** Patreon says it is the deemed supplier to patrons. Under EU rules (Art. 9a Reg. 282/2011, mirrored in Polish art. 8 ust. 2a VAT Act, the "komis usług" fiction) that would mean the creator supplies to Patreon and Patreon's margin is not a separate service to the creator. On that reading there is arguably no import of services on the fees, and the NP invoice could be for the **net** amount. Patreon's Terms and Help Center, and Polish practice, instead frame the fees as services Patreon sells to creators, taxable by reverse charge. The legal construct and the CJEU Fenix case (C-695/20) are background knowledge, not re-verified in this session. No KIS ruling on Patreon specifically resolving this was found. Whichever model is chosen, the VAT invoice amount and the PIT revenue figure should follow it consistently.
- Payment-processing and currency-conversion fees could arguably be exempt financial services if treated as separate imported services. That would mean no VAT, but the import is still reported. This is unverified; it is a point for the accountant.

### Gaps
- No KIS individual interpretation specifically naming Patreon on VAT (art. 28b, NP, import of services on fees) was found. Searches of commentary returned none, and eureka.mf.gov.pl could not be queried directly.
- Patreon, Inc.'s US EIN was not found in public sources, and neither were VAT numbers of Patreon Ireland Limited / Patreon DLC Limited. It is unclear whether, after the 2026 Terms change, EU VAT on fans is still declared under the non-Union scheme number EU372009942. The Help Center (updated 2026-09-24) still lists that number.
- Not verified from an official MF/KSeF source in this session: whether a buyer without Polish NIP or EU VAT ID is coded in FA(3) with a "no ID" flag, and the exact FA(3) rate code ("np I"/"np II" vs the "NP1/NP2" naming used by Prawo.pl).
- Whether Patreon actually adds VAT to fees for Polish creators without a VAT ID could not be verified. The Sales Tax Requirements article lists the EU, with Poland in its EU VAT list, for patron sales; tax-on-fees countries are only said to be "generally aligned" with those. Check the "taxes on fees" line in the creator's own Earnings dashboard.

## 2. Income tax: gross vs net revenue, deductibility of Patreon fees, ryczałt, revenue date, NBP conversion, US withholding (W-8BEN / 1042-S)

### Takeaway
Patreon withholds no income tax: no Polish PIT and no US withholding once a W-8BEN/W-8BEN-E is on file. The creator reports everything in Poland. Polish practitioners treat Patreon fees as deductible costs under liniowy/skala and non-deductible under ryczałt, which implies taxing the gross amount (excluding the VAT Patreon collects). A "net" model, consistent with the deemed-supplier logic, is also defensible. No Patreon-specific KIS ruling settles which one applies, and under ryczałt the difference is material. Patreon's monthly data supports both, since the Earnings page and CSVs show gross, fees and net. Currency handling is simplest if the Patreon creator/payout currency is PLN.

### Inferences
- **Liniowy / skala:** the defensible and data-supported default is revenue = Patreon "gross" (membership + shop amounts excluding VAT/sales tax collected by Patreon), with platform fee, processing fee, taxes on fees, currency conversion fee, Apple App Store fee and payout fee as KPiR costs. DAC7 reports gross and fees separately, so KAS will see the gross figure. A net-revenue approach gives the same taxable income here; only the presentation differs.
- **Ryczałt:** gross vs net changes the tax base by roughly 13–16% of revenue (10% platform + ~3.4% processing, +2.5% conversion if members pay in another currency, or 30% on iOS in-app purchases). The accountant should take an explicit position, or the user should consider an individual interpretation. The rate depends on the PKWiU classification of what patrons get (access to digital content, posts, etc.), not on the SaaS business. If no Patreon-specific rate is confirmed, this is a question for the accountant.
- **Revenue date and FX:** if the Patreon payout currency is **PLN**, Patreon's earnings and CSVs are already in PLN. No art. 11a NBP conversion is needed for revenue, and payouts by local PLN bank transfer cause no exchange differences. If the currency is USD/EUR, the accountant must choose either per-transaction conversion (NBP rate from the day before each patron charge, which needs the per-patron transaction CSV) or a monthly-settlement approach (art. 14 ust. 1e PIT, revenue on the last day of the monthly settlement period if invoiced monthly, which needs only the monthly totals). Under KPiR, exchange differences arise at payout. The art. 14 ust. 1c/1e and art. 11a mechanics are general PIT rules, not re-verified here and covered by other researchers.
- The W-8BEN is a payout prerequisite, not a recurring document. Keep a copy, and keep the TIN, address and VAT-ID data current, because the same form feeds DAC7.

### Gaps
- No Patreon-specific KIS interpretation on gross vs net PIT revenue, or on the ryczałt rate for Patreon memberships, was found.
- No evidence that Patreon issues **Form 1042-S** to non-US creators. Its documentation mentions only 1099-K (US) and a yearly gross-earnings figure. The unverified assumption is that none is issued while a valid W-8BEN is on file.
- Whether Patreon's own "average exchange rate from the day before" can be reconciled to NBP rates was not examined. It differs by definition.

## 3. How Polish creators and accountants handle Patreon in practice (interpretations, blogs, documents used)

### Takeaway
No KIS interpretation naming Patreon was found. Polish guidance comes from accounting blogs (2024–2026) and from interpretations on the Polish platform Patronite, which mainly address whether patron payments are gifts; for a JDG that gives benefits, they are not. The practical document set is: Patreon dashboard statements/CSVs, payout confirmations, bank/PayPal/Payoneer statements, and, if invoicing is adopted, a monthly invoice to Patreon, Inc. Patreon does not issue invoices or self-billing documents to creators. It tells creators that the earnings data is sufficient and that they can build their own statement from the Payouts page.

### Cited Findings
- Patreon on documentation: "Patreon will email receipts to members... We will send you an annual email tallying up all earnings. If you earn more than $600 a year and are a US-based creator, we will send tax documents to you. Yet, as a person running a small business, you need to keep records and documents and do your own bookkeeping." Also: "if your accountant is asking for a list of how much you earned per month, with Patreon's logo and snail mail address, you can take the data from your Payouts page and make the document." (updated 2026-10-16) — [Patreon: What are my responsibilities as a creator?](https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/115004484326-What-are-my-responsibilities-as-a-Patreon-creator)
- Q: "How do I report my Patreon income to the tax authorities without a proper invoice?" A: "We are working on improved invoices, but in the meantime, all of the information required to report your income is readily available in the 'earnings' tab of your creator homepage." Q: "My tax advisor also doesn't know how to report income from Patreon." A: "we are unable to provide specific tax advice." — [Patreon: EU Creator FAQ](https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/360047578411-EU-Creator-Frequently-Asked-Tax-Questions)
- Documents to keep: monthly statements from the Patreon dashboard, payout confirmations, fee invoices, invoices issued to Patreon. Records "must match the platform's monthly statements" because of DAC7. — [KontoTwórcy.pl (updated 10.09.2026, Dawid Łazarz)](https://kontotworcy.pl/poradnik/patreon-podatek/)
- Documents the accountant needs: "Raporty wpłat i wypłat z platform, wyciągi z konta, PayPal czy Payoneer oraz faktury za zakupy, jeśli rozliczasz koszty". Patreon requires a W-8BEN. — [General Office](https://generaloffice.pl/rozliczenia-podatkowe-patronite-patreon/)
- Another accounting office (29.06.2024): keep proof of every payment and information about the benefits given in return. Patreon payments with benefits are business revenue. Pure donations without benefits could be gifts, outside VAT. It does not discuss art. 28b, invoices or fees. — [NEX biuro rachunkowe: Jak rozliczyć podatkowo Patreon?](https://www.nex.katowice.pl/post/jak-rozliczy%C4%87-podatkowo-patreon)
- Patronite line (not Patreon): the 2021 interpretations 0111-KDIB2-2.4015.96.2021.2.MM and 0111-KDIB2-3.4015.187.2021.3.ŁSZ treated payments as donations; 2024 0111-KDIB2-2.4015.109.2024.3.PB said they "mogą być traktowane jako darowizny". The **6 Oct 2025 interpretation 0111-KDIB2-2.4015.59.2022.12.PB** treats payments as donations only where the donor can be identified, and anonymous/pseudonymous payments as PIT income. It was issued after WSA Wrocław and NSA rulings forced a substantive answer. KPMG notes that Patreon is not covered. — [KPMG: Więcej pewności podatkowej dla twórców internetowych (Oct 2025)](https://kpmg.com/pl/pl/wiedza/podatki/wiecej-pewnosci-podatkowej-dla-tworcow-internetowych-blog-podatkowy.html)
- KontoTwórcy cites a September 2025 interpretation on crowdfunding portals and an "NSA judgment of 7 July 2026", both without signature or case number. It concludes that on Patreon an equivalent (benefit) usually exists, so the donation question rarely arises. — [KontoTwórcy.pl](https://kontotworcy.pl/poradnik/patreon-podatek/)

### Inferences
- For this user (JDG; Patreon tiers presumably give benefits), the gift/Patronite debate is irrelevant: Patreon income is business revenue of the JDG.
- Since Patreon issues no invoices, self-billing documents or fee invoices to creators, the fee side of any "import usług" must be documented with the creator's own Earnings statement/CSV. KontoTwórcy's "fee invoices" do not appear to exist; Patreon only says taxes on fees are "shown in your creator earnings dashboard".
- Quality issues in Polish blog sources. KontoTwórcy's "reverse charge" annotation for a US buyer conflicts with Prawo.pl's NP/art. 28b approach. Its DAC7 threshold "30 transakcji lub 2000 euro" is not in Patreon's DAC7 article, which says "the total earnings of a creator offering any of the reportable benefits will be reported". From general knowledge, that threshold is the DAC7 de minimis for sales of goods, not services. Its unnumbered court citations are unverified.

### Gaps
- No individual interpretation (eureka.mf.gov.pl) mentioning "Patreon" was found. Eureka could not be queried programmatically, and web searches of commentary returned none. A direct keyword search for "Patreon" in eureka is recommended before relying on any position.
- No Polish forum thread with a verified, detailed accountant workflow for Patreon (e.g., the exact monthly invoice amount basis) was found.

## 4. Patreon creator reports and exports (2025–2026): what exists, where, and what it contains

### Takeaway
Everything for accounting lives in **Creator studio > Payouts > Documents** (`patreon.com/payouts/documents`) and on the **Earnings** page (`patreon.com/insights/earnings`). Available items: monthly earnings CSV, per-patron transaction CSV, withdrawal (payout) history CSV, refund CSV, VAT/sales-tax CSVs by country and US state, a yearly gross figure, and a Proof of Income letter on Patreon letterhead. The Earnings page shows gross, fees (platform, payment fees incl. taxes on fees and conversion), refunds, declines and net per month. Payout fees are not in the Earnings breakdown and must be taken from the withdrawal record. Exact CSV column headers are not published.

### Cited Findings
- The Payouts dashboard (`patreon.com/payouts/withdrawal`) is "where you can withdraw ... review your payout withdrawal history, and download essential earnings and tax CSV files". All earnings, payouts, sales-tax and income-tax CSVs are in **Payouts > Documents** (`patreon.com/payouts/documents`). Contents: **Withdrawal history CSV** ("A complete history of all payouts"); **Earnings breakdown by month CSV** ("The total amount we've processed for you in each month"); **Patron transaction history CSV** ("A complete history of processed payments for each of your patrons"); **Refund history CSV**; **Income Tax** ("Your gross earnings yearly", plus 1099-K for US); **Sales Tax: United States (by state) CSV**; **Sales Tax: Rest of world (by country)** ("taxes collected and paid on your patrons' behalf by country"). The on-page payout history table shows Date, Description, Destination, Amount, Status. (updated 2026-10-07) — [Patreon: Payouts Dashboard](https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/360058429051-Payouts-Dashboard)
- VAT records: Creator studio > Payouts > Documents > Sales tax section. "Click the Download button next to the document to get a breakdown of sales tax, including VAT", listed by member country. — [Patreon: How VAT works for creators](https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/205259549-How-VAT-works-for-creators-on-Patreon)
- "Use the Earnings page for an accurate record of your gross and net income (after fees) for each month." The Overview on the public page is only an estimate. Filters: date range (6 months, 12 months, all time, custom), earnings type (All/Membership/Shop), **Currency toggle (early access)**, free/paid view, daily/monthly view, tier, billing cadence. Columns: Membership/Shop (Web & Android)/Shop (iOS) **before** fees; Declines; Refunds ("only includes the creator's share"; Patreon's fee, processing fees and taxes are refunded by Patreon; fraud refunds excluded); Membership/Shop/Shop (iOS) **after** fees. There is "a breakdown showing things like platform fees, refunds, declines, and your final net earnings". (new article created 2026-07-03) — [Patreon: Earnings Insights](https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/47114923757197-Earnings-Insights)
- "Payment processing fees and taxes on fees will be included under the Payment Fees column" of the Earnings dashboard. The currency conversion fee is "shown under the Payment Fees column". — [Patreon: Creator fees FAQ](https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/22581195376909-Creator-fees-FAQ); [Patreon: Creator fees overview](https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/11111747095181-Creator-fees-overview)
- "You can see your fees in Earnings Insights, which updates every 4 hours. For a full per-transaction breakdown, download the monthly CSV from the Documents section of your Payouts page." Separately: "Your payout fee is not listed in the breakdown of fees within your creator earnings dashboard." — [Patreon: Why is my payout less than I expected?](https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/204606105-Why-is-my-payout-less-than-I-expected); [Patreon: Creator fees overview](https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/11111747095181-Creator-fees-overview)
- The "Member Transaction History" CSV (Payouts > Documents) can be sorted by fans' pay-in **currency**. — [Patreon: Payouts guide for creators outside the US](https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/39694936541965-Payouts-guide-for-creators-outside-of-the-US)
- "Use the Earnings dashboard, and Payouts reports for your monthly totals. These reports are reliable records of what we've processed for you in a given month." Dashboard data refreshes every 4 hours. — [Patreon: Understand the numbers listed on my Patreon](https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/206197906-Understand-the-numbers-listed-on-my-Patreon)
- **Proof of Income letter** (feature added Sept 2025): an "automatically generated document on official Patreon letterhead" with creator name, total earnings for the selected calendar year (excluding refunds), estimated monthly earnings and Patreon's company details. Generated anytime from Payouts > Documents > Proof of Income. — [Patreon: Proof of Income letter](https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/39604639869709-How-do-I-request-a-Proof-of-Income-letter)
- Annual email "tallying up all earnings". — [Patreon: creator responsibilities](https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/115004484326-What-are-my-responsibilities-as-a-Patreon-creator)
- Pending periods affect when earnings become withdrawable, not when they are shown: Commerce (Web/Android) up to 7 days; iOS in-app purchases up to 75 days; ticketed lives 7 days after the event. — [Patreon: How payouts work](https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/208656246-How-payouts-work)
- Column names seen only in a search-result snippet of an open-source ledger importer, not opened or verified and possibly outdated: "Creator Platform Fee", "Creator Payment Processing Fee", "Creator Currency Conversion Fee", "Creator Share", "Date", "Event Type", "Creator Currency", "Event ID", "Patron Email", "Patron Name", "Patron User ID". A patron report had "Pledge $", "Lifetime $", "Tier", "Charge Status", "User ID". — [SFC import2ledger changeset](https://k.sfconservancy.org/NPO-Accounting/import2ledger/changeset/0eb014aeeb2ee589dbb3bef28e3cbd15023cd174?context=24)

### Inferences
- **Minimal monthly handover for the accountant:**
  1. Earnings for the month: the Earnings page monthly row or the "Earnings breakdown by month" CSV, giving gross, fees, refunds and net. This is the basis for the invoice to Patreon, Inc. and for PIT revenue/costs.
  2. Withdrawal history CSV plus the bank/PayPal/Payoneer statement, for payouts and payout fees and for reconciliation.
  3. Sales Tax "Rest of world (by country)" CSV, as evidence that VAT on patrons was charged by Patreon in its own name.
  4. Optionally, the Patron/Member transaction history CSV, needed only for per-transaction FX conversion or an audit trail.
- The patron CSV contains member personal data, and the Terms bind creators to Patreon's Data Processing Agreement. Sharing aggregates rather than per-patron files is the privacy-minimal option.
- Yearly: the Proof of Income letter, a copy of the W-8BEN, and awareness of the January DAC7 report.

### Gaps
- Patreon publishes no official column list for any of the CSVs. Whether the monthly CSV has a separate VAT/"taxes on fees" column, and in which currency per row, must be checked on a real export.
- No scheduled or automatic e-mail delivery of monthly CSV statements was found. Only an annual earnings e-mail and on-demand downloads are documented.

## 5. Automation: Patreon API v2 capabilities, webhooks, rate limits, deprecations, and the least-manual path

### Takeaway
The Patreon API v2 exposes campaigns, members, tiers, posts, lives and webhooks. It does **not** expose earnings, fees, VAT/taxes, net amounts, balances or payouts: the API docs do not contain the word "payout". The only money data is per-member and per-charge amounts in the **patron's currency** (Member, Pledge Event), which is not enough for bookkeeping. A Go script could at best reconstruct gross charges; fees, VAT, net, FX and payouts would still need the manually downloaded CSVs. API v1 was retired on 7 October 2026. The least-manual workable process is a monthly manual download of 2–4 CSVs from Payouts > Documents, optionally fed into a small local parser or summary script.

### Cited Findings
- Endpoints documented for v2 include `/api/oauth2/v2/identity`, `/campaigns`, `/campaigns/{campaign_id}`, `/campaigns/{campaign_id}/members`, `/members/{id}`, `/posts/{id}`, webhooks (`/api/oauth2/v2/webhooks`) and Live endpoints (added 2026-03-11). — [Patreon API docs](https://docs.patreon.com/)
- Scopes: identity, identity[email], identity.memberships, campaigns, w:campaigns.webhook, campaigns.lives, w:campaigns.lives, campaigns.members, campaigns.members[email], campaigns.members.address, campaigns.posts. The client creator's own token automatically has all v2 scopes. — [Patreon API docs](https://docs.patreon.com/)
- Member attributes: campaign_lifetime_support_cents ("in the campaign's currency"), currently_entitled_amount_cents, email, full_name, is_free_trial, is_gifted, last_charge_date, last_charge_status, next_charge_date, note, patron_status, pledge_cadence, pledge_relationship_start, will_pay_amount_cents. Relationship **pledge_history** (Pledge Event) has amount_cents ("in the currency in which the patron paid"), currency_code, date, payment_status (Paid, Declined, Deleted, Pending, Refunded, Fraud, Refunded by Patreon, Other, Partially Refunded, Free Trial, Refund Pending, Refund Declined), pledge_payment_status, tier_id, tier_title, type (pledge_start, pledge_upgrade, pledge_downgrade, pledge_delete, subscription). Pages hold 1,000 members, or 500 when pledge events are included, with cursor pagination. — [Patreon API docs](https://docs.patreon.com/)
- "if you use the amount_cents on the Member, PledgeEvent or Pledge resources, the value returned is always in the patron's currency, not the reward tier or campaign currency." — [Patreon: Currency updates to the Patreon API](https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/360047287991-Currency-updates-to-the-Patreon-API)
- The docs mention no payout/balance/fee resource. A keyword scan of docs.patreon.com found 0 occurrences of "payout"; "earnings" appears only as the campaign flag `show_earnings` and similar. — [Patreon API docs](https://docs.patreon.com/)
- Webhooks v2 triggers: members:create, members:update ("Includes updates on payment charging events"), members:delete, members:pledge:create, members:pledge:update, members:pledge:delete, posts:publish, posts:update, posts:delete. Headers X-Patreon-Event and X-Patreon-Signature (HEX HMAC-MD5 of the body with the webhook secret). Payload data is limited by the webhook's scopes. — [Patreon API docs](https://docs.patreon.com/)
- Rate limits (updated 2024-10-14; edge limits added 2025-07-04): client up to 100 requests / 2 s; access token up to 100 requests / minute; HTTP 429 with `retry_after_seconds`. More than 2,000 HTTP 4xx in 10 minutes triggers a 30-minute block. A User-Agent header is required or calls may get 403. — [Patreon API docs](https://docs.patreon.com/)
- Deprecations and changes: "Public API v1 is retiring on October 7, 2026. Calls to v1 will stop working after that date", with an extension to **20 January 2027** if requested by 7 Oct. pledges:* webhook triggers stop at the same time. Creation of new v1 clients was restricted 2026-03-25. **Member identity masking (2026-05-26):** names, image URLs and vanity can be null if a member hides their identity. Campaign `name` and `currency` attributes were added 2026-03-09. — [Patreon API docs](https://docs.patreon.com/)
- Support posture: "Existing API functionality will continue to be supported and maintained ... we will have limited capacity in the near term to respond to questions through the developer forum." — [Patreon API docs](https://docs.patreon.com/)

### Inferences
- **API automation of accounting data is not possible.** A script can list each member's charge events (gross, patron currency, date, status) via `campaigns/{id}/members?include=pledge_history`. It cannot get Patreon's fees, the conversion into the creator currency (Patreon uses its own "average exchange rate from the day before"), VAT, net, the balance or payouts. A reconstruction would not tie out to Patreon's statements.
- **Least-manual workflow:** once a month (after the 5th, when automatic payouts and most 1st-of-month charges have settled), log in and download from Payouts > Documents the Earnings-by-month, Withdrawal-history and Sales-tax (rest of world) CSVs, plus the member transaction CSV only if needed. Optionally run a small local script that sums the month and adds NBP rates if the currency is not PLN. Then send the files to the accountant. Scraping the logged-in dashboard is fragile, and the Terms prohibit using Patreon "in a technical way" that abuses it; I'd advise against it.
- If a Go script is written for Stripe anyway, the Patreon part should be a CSV-ingest step, not an API client. The Earnings CSV is per month, so one file covers a month.

### Gaps
- No Patreon API endpoint, export API or scheduled report for earnings, fees or payouts was found. No roadmap item suggesting one was found either.
- Exact CSV headers (needed for a parser) are undocumented; see section 4.

## 6. Payout methods, currencies, fees and timing, and how payouts appear on the user's side

### Takeaway
For a Polish creator, payouts can go by local bank transfer in **PLN or EUR (processed by Payoneer)**, PayPal, or a Payoneer Wallet. Stripe direct deposit is US-only. With PLN as creator currency, processing fees are 3.4% + 1.75 PLN per payment and the bank payout fee is 2 zł (Payoneer 3 zł, PayPal 5 zł). Patrons paying in another currency incur a 2.5% conversion fee. Automatic payouts run on the 5th of each month for the full available balance and arrive in about 1–5 business days. Payouts are lump sums of the available balance, so they do not equal a month's earnings and need the withdrawal CSV to reconcile.

### Cited Findings
- Options by country: "Bank transfer (processed by Stripe) – available for creators based in the U.S"; "Bank transfer (processed by Payoneer) – available to creators who reside outside of the U.S"; PayPal; Payoneer Wallet (international only). — [Patreon: How payouts work](https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/208656246-How-payouts-work)
- Poland is listed for local-currency bank transfer in **PLN, EUR**, and for USD-to-local transfer in PLN. Local transfer: "Patreon sends your funds in your local currency ... straight to your bank account", no conversion at payout, minimum $10 or local equivalent. USD-to-local: 1.55% + $0.25. USD-to-USD account (e.g., Wise): $0.50. No Payoneer account is needed for bank transfers. (article created 2025-09-19, updated 2026-10-17) — [Patreon: Payouts guide for creators outside the US](https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/39694936541965-Payouts-guide-for-creators-outside-of-the-US)
- Payout fees per payout in **PLN**: bank transfer **2 zł**, Payoneer **3 zł**, PayPal **5.00 zł**. EUR: €0.50 / €0.75 / €1.00. USD for non-US creators: PayPal 1% (min $0.25, cap $20, $10 minimum payout); Payoneer Wallet $1.00 ($25 minimum). PayPal and Payoneer may add their own withdrawal or FX fees. — [Patreon: Creator fees overview](https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/11111747095181-Creator-fees-overview)
- Platform fee: the **standard 10% plan + applicable taxes** applies to creators who publish after **4 Aug 2025**, or who unpublish and republish. Legacy: Founders 5% (pre-7 May 2019), Pro 8%, Pro+Merch 11%. Standard-plan processing for PLN payout currency: **3.4% + PLN 1.75**; legacy PLN: 5% + PLN 0.70 (≤ PLN 15) or 3.4% + PLN 1.75 (> PLN 15). USD: 2.9% + $0.30 (cards); PayPal/Venmo non-US 3.9% + $0.30. Currency conversion fee **2.5%** on payments in a currency other than the payout currency. **Apple iOS in-app purchases carry a 30% App Store fee** instead of Patreon processing (memberships via IAP since Nov 2024; US fans may use mobile web checkout). Patreon may raise iOS prices by default to offset it. — [Patreon: Creator fees overview](https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/11111747095181-Creator-fees-overview); [Patreon: iOS IAP and switching to subscription billing](https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/27992299290637-iOS-in-app-purchases-and-switching-to-subscription-billing)
- Timing: "When enabled, automatic payouts are initiated on the 5th of each month, as long as you have a positive balance, and send your full available balance." Manual payouts are allowed once every 24 hours and "typically [take] 1-5 days"; some may take up to 10 business days for volume checks. There is a 5-day payout hold after adding or changing a payout method. "Funds in your Patreon balance already reflect the platform fee, payment processing fees, and any currency conversion fees", and the payout fee is deducted at transfer. — [Patreon: How payouts work](https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/208656246-How-payouts-work); [Patreon: Paying out your earnings](https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/203913499-Paying-out-your-earnings)
- Currency: the creator currency can be changed once every 31 days, only with a $0 available balance (pending iOS balances are fine). After a change the payout method must be re-added, which triggers a 5-day lock. Founders creators may lose Founders processing rates. — [Patreon: Setting my creator currency FAQ](https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/360039539851-Setting-my-creator-currency-FAQ); [Patreon: Changing your payout currency](https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/360048258772-Changing-your-payout-currency)
- Other balance effects: merch costs deducted on the 4th; your own memberships to other creators are subtracted from your payout; refunds come out of the creator balance and may make it negative. — [Patreon: Why is my payout less than I expected?](https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/204606105-Why-is-my-payout-less-than-I-expected); [Patreon: creator responsibilities](https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/115004484326-What-are-my-responsibilities-as-a-Patreon-creator)
- DAC7 data includes the "Financial account identifier(s) i.e. identifier of the account to which payments are made to you (PayPal, Stripe, etc.)". — [Patreon: DAC7](https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/21712170817293-EU-Creator-Introduction-to-income-reporting-requirements-DAC7)

### Inferences
- Simplest accounting setup: **creator currency PLN + local PLN bank transfer + automatic payouts on the 5th**. Revenue data is in PLN, there is no FX at payout, the fee is a flat 2 zł, and each month ends with one bank receipt that matches a Withdrawal-history row. The trade-offs: patrons paying USD/EUR trigger the 2.5% conversion fee, and switching currency needs a zero balance and a 5-day payout lock.
- With PayPal, the payout lands in the PayPal balance. PayPal is another payment intermediary whose own statements and FX fees the accountant would need (the Stripe/PayPal researchers cover PayPal statements).
- A payout is a balance transfer that settles the receivable from Patreon, Inc. It is not the revenue event. The monthly invoice/revenue amount comes from the Earnings statement, and the bank receipt is matched against it, with differences from timing, refunds, pending iOS funds and payout fees.

### Gaps
- The sender name and reference that appear on a Polish bank statement for a "Bank transfer (processed by Payoneer)" payout were not found. Whether it shows "Patreon", "Payoneer" or a correspondent bank needs checking on a real transfer.
- Whether Patreon provides a per-payout remittance advice or confirmation e-mail with the amount and fee was not verified. The Withdrawal history CSV and the on-page table are documented; the e-mail is not.
